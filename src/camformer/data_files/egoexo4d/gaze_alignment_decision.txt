Gaze alignment decision, 2026-10-20 (Valerio Costa). Option (b).

Decision: proceed on the documented timestamps, no shift applied. Basis: the Aria MPS documentation gives
closed_loop_trajectory and general_eye_gaze tracking_timestamp_us both in device time, so gaze and pose are assumed
to share a clock.

What the checks showed (docs/gaze.md, "Checks before training"; results/gaze_checks.md; job 709871): the eye-head
lag peaks at +100 to +150 ms in both raw 10 Hz gaze and our 20 Hz features (the pipeline adds nothing); per take
+90 to +250 ms (median +140), every take positive, with differences by activity on a selected subset of takes. At
10 Hz the vestibulo-ocular reflex cannot be isolated (eye/head rate ratio -0.03 against about -1 expected). The lag
could be eyes leading the head or a timing offset; the checks cannot tell them apart. The pre-set alignment
criterion stays failed.
